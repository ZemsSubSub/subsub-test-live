SubSub Live — clickable prototype

Static prototype of the Live section: HTML + vanilla JS, no backend.

Data is hard-coded, asynchronous steps are simulated with timers, and state lives in your browser's localStorage. Uploads, conversion, YouTube connection and payments are imitated. Other sections of the product are not part of this prototype.
